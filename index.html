Research Profile

I am an MSc Mathematical Sciences researcher (University of Cape Coast / African Institute for Mathematical Sciences – Ghana) and Tutorial Fellow at Jaramogi Oginga Odinga University of Science and Technology, with a rigorous foundation in real analysis, probability theory, linear algebra, mathematical proofs, statistics, and scientific computing with Python[cite: 2, 3].

My research objective is to develop a rigorous mathematical understanding of learning algorithms—particularly their optimisation and training dynamics, high-dimensional behaviour, generalisation, uncertainty, and architectural mechanisms[cite: 2].
