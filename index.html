About Me

Erick Omondi Aketch is a mathematician and data scientist holding an MSc in Mathematical Sciences and a Certificate in Data Science. His work bridges theoretical mathematics—including graph theory and dynamical systems—with applied statistical inference and Python-based machine learning.

His research spans epidemiological modeling of infectious disease transmission dynamics, quantitative financial engineering, and structural graph parameters such as the Wiener index on posets of trees.
